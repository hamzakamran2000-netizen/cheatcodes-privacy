1. Summary

  • The App has no accounts, sign-ups, or logins. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or location.

2. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following locally on your device so it works offline and remembers your choices. This information is not sent to us.

  • Your selected app language.
  • A cached copy of the combo / cheat-code list.
  • Your privacy and advertising consent choices (stored by Google’s consent tools).

3. Content downloads

To keep the combo list up to date, the App downloads a data file from our content host. Like any web server, the host may automatically process your IP address and basic request information (such as time and device type) to deliver the file and keep the service secure. We do not use this to identify you.

  • Device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), where permitted.

This data is used to show ads, measure their performance, prevent fraud, and, if you allow it, personalise ads. Google’s processing is governed by Google’s own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Your choices and consent

  • European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland: the first time you open the App, a consent form lets you accept or decline personalised advertising and related data processing. You can change your choice at any time from About → Privacy settings in the App.

If you decline personalised ads, you may still see non-personalised ads, which are based on context (such as the App itself) rather than your profile.
